# Technify University — Late Submission Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all **assignments, projects, lab reports, and coursework submissions** across undergraduate and graduate programs at Technify University.

## 2. Standard Submission Deadlines

- All assignments must be submitted by **11:59 PM** on the due date specified by the course instructor.
- Submissions are accepted via the university LMS portal unless the instructor specifies otherwise.
- Late submissions without approved extensions will receive grade penalties as defined below.

## 3. Late Submission Penalties

| Days Late | Penalty |
|-----------|---------|
| 1 day | 10% deduction from total marks |
| 2 days | 20% deduction from total marks |
| 3 days | 30% deduction from total marks |
| 4-7 days | 50% deduction from total marks |
| More than 7 days | **Zero marks** (not accepted) |

## 4. Valid Extensions

Extensions may be granted only for:
- **Medical emergencies** (medical certificate required within 48 hours)
- **Bereavement** (immediate family — up to 3 days extension)
- **University-sanctioned events** (official letter from the department required)

Extension requests must be submitted **before the original deadline** via email to the course instructor with supporting documentation.

## 5. Repeated Late Submissions

Students with **three or more late submissions** in a single course during one semester will receive a formal warning and may be referred to the academic advisor.

## 6. Faculty Discretion

Course instructors may apply stricter deadlines for time-sensitive assessments (e.g., in-class presentations, group projects). The instructor's published rubric takes precedence for that assessment.
