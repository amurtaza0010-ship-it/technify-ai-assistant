# Technify University — Academic Integrity Policy

## 1. Purpose

Technify University is committed to maintaining the highest standards of academic honesty. All students, faculty, and staff are expected to uphold integrity in all academic work.

## 2. Definitions of Academic Misconduct

The following actions constitute academic misconduct:

### Plagiarism
- Submitting another person's work, ideas, or words without proper citation.
- Using AI-generated content without disclosure when prohibited by the instructor.
- Self-plagiarism: re-submitting previously graded work without permission.

### Cheating
- Using unauthorized materials during exams or quizzes.
- Copying from another student during an assessment.
- Sharing exam questions or answers with other students.

### Fabrication and Falsification
- Inventing data, sources, or experimental results.
- Altering grades, transcripts, or official documents.

### Collusion
- Unauthorized collaboration on individual assignments.
- Paying a third party to complete coursework (contract cheating).

## 3. Penalties

| Offense Level | Penalty |
|---------------|---------|
| First minor offense | Warning + zero on the assignment |
| First major offense | **F grade** in the course + disciplinary record |
| Second offense | **Suspension for one semester** |
| Third offense | **Expulsion** from the university |

## 4. Reporting and Investigation

- Faculty members must report suspected misconduct to the **Academic Integrity Committee** within 5 working days.
- Students accused of misconduct have the right to a **formal hearing** within 14 days.
- Both parties may present evidence and witnesses.

## 5. Student Responsibilities

- Complete the mandatory **Academic Integrity Orientation** in the first semester.
- Cite all sources using the approved citation style (APA/IEEE as specified by the department).
- Report suspected misconduct to the course instructor or the Dean of Students.

## 6. Use of AI Tools

- AI tools may be used only when explicitly permitted by the course instructor.
- When permitted, students must **disclose AI usage** in their submission.
- Submitting AI-generated work as original without disclosure is treated as plagiarism.
