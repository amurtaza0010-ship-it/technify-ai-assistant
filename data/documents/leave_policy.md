# Technify University — Leave Policy

## 1. Types of Leave

Technify University recognizes the following leave categories for enrolled students:

| Leave Type | Maximum Duration | Documentation Required |
|------------|------------------|------------------------|
| Medical Leave | Up to 4 weeks per semester | Medical certificate from registered hospital |
| Bereavement Leave | Up to 5 days | Application with proof of relationship |
| Emergency Leave | Up to 3 days | Written application to course advisor |
| Semester Leave (Freeze) | One full semester | Formal application to Registrar |
| Maternity/Paternity Leave | As per labor policy | Official medical documentation |

## 2. Application Process

1. Submit a **Leave Application Form** via the student portal at least **3 working days** before the leave start date (except emergencies).
2. Notify all course instructors via email.
3. Obtain approval from the **Department Advisor**.
4. For semester leave, obtain additional approval from the **Registrar's Office**.

## 3. Attendance During Leave

- Approved medical and bereavement leave days are **exempt from attendance penalties**.
- Students must submit supporting documents within **48 hours** of returning to campus.
- Unapproved absences count toward the attendance deficit and may affect exam eligibility.

## 4. Academic Impact

- Students on approved semester leave retain their enrollment status but may not sit in exams during the leave period.
- Course registration for the following semester must be completed before the leave is approved.
- Financial aid and scholarship eligibility may be affected — consult the Finance Office.

## 5. Return from Leave

- Students returning from semester leave must submit a **Rejoining Application** before the add/drop deadline.
- Medical clearance may be required for extended medical leave exceeding 4 weeks.

## 6. Faculty Leave

Faculty members must follow the separate **Faculty Leave Policy** administered by the HR Department. Student-facing queries about faculty absence should be directed to the department office.
